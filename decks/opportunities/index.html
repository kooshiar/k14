Trap for the smallest forever chemical (TFA)

TFA is the smallest “forever chemical”. It comes from pesticides, refrigerant gases and industry, falls with rain, and never breaks down. It is now in rivers, tap water and even bottled water in Europe, US states are starting to test for it, and levels keep rising. Normal water filters can’t catch it because it is tiny and dissolves too easily. Only expensive reverse osmosis works today. We are designing a cheap filter material that grabs TFA and lets everything else in the water pass.
